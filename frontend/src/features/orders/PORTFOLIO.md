Do not publish it as an API. No proposed `/v1/orders/portfolios/compare` call is implemented. No aggregation from P1/Core stands in for joint P2 verification.

Future owner work: verify a registered read contract, permission/redaction policy, source readers, canonical order/request/scenario identities and versions, current operational snapshot, expiry, joint P2 validation, objective/model/policy identifiers, price/cost provenance and recommendation authority.
