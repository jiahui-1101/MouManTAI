## Current status and next safe task

This branch implements a deterministic, side-effect-free mapper and candidate interface. It is locally tested and pushed in open Draft PR #50, but not merged or integrated. Independent reviews led to structured disclosure records that bind tenant/customer/context/version/request/terms revision/hash/source version/disclosure time, currentness lookup and adapter authorities; preservation of the P1 association version used; suppression of unverified order IDs and commercial request/source/disclosure references on failure results; proposed-revision consistency; and named-timezone timestamp validation. The mapper compares these claims to canonical terms but cannot authenticate its internal adapter or prove source freshness after return. Missing price authority, verified/current disclosure, complete terms, identity, review, source records, or matching versions fails closed. It does not create an order request or customer assent.

Next safe Backend A task after this PR: design and implement a tenant-scoped read adapter that resolves authoritative P1 context/version/review/message/association records and approved price catalog/quote plus disclosure evidence; review its source-of-truth contract with P1/P5 and Backend B before connecting it to any API. A durable OrderRequest and migration remain a separate owner-reviewed slice.
